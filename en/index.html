Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your phone, and in your own iCloud (iPhone, iPad) or Google (Android) backup. No account is needed.

My partner has an Android phone and I have an iPhone: will it work?

Yes. Sharing with your partner works between iPhone and Android. Your partner scans the invitation QR code with their camera; if they don’t have the app, they’re guided to the App Store or Google Play, then the project is added.

I’m doing some of the work myself: is the app right for me?

Yes. Record who does each stage, separate materials from labour, log your returns to the store and, with Plus, compare your purchase prices and your savings against contractors’ quotes.

Does invoice reading send my invoices anywhere?

No. Automatic reading happens on your phone. No invoice is sent to an outside service to be read.

How do I cancel the subscription?

iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Android: Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. The lifetime purchase has nothing to cancel.
