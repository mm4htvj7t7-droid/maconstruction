Costitecture Privacy Policy

Effective from 18 November 2026. Previous version: 21 October 2026.

Translated version of the French original. View the French version

This policy explains what the Costitecture app does with your data, on iPhone, iPad and Android. Depending on the language, the app may have a different name: MaConstruction (French), Costitecture (English), CuentaObra (Spanish), Obraçamento (Portuguese), Ziegeld (German), ContoCantiere (Italian), Stenen & Centen (Dutch). This policy applies to all its versions.

Summary

  • No account to create. No advertising. No advertising trackers. We do not sell any data.
  • Your projects stay with you: on your phone, and in your own iCloud or Google backup. We have no access to them.
  • Sharing with your partner: an end-to-end encrypted copy passes through our server. Neither we nor the hosting provider can read it.
  • Anonymous statistics off by default. The app asks you a single question. They are sent only if you answer “Share”.
  • Invoice reading on the phone: no image is sent to an outside service to be read.
  • No advertising, no tracking.
  • Purchases handled by Apple or Google. We see neither your name nor your payment method.

3. What the app does, processing by processing

3.1 What you enter stays on your phone

Data concerned. Projects, stages, budget, expenses, credit notes, payments due, quotes, invoices, photos, documents, contractors and their contact details, shopping list (items, noted prices, references, links, delivery costs), the project’s country and currency.

  • Everything is stored in the app, on your device.
  • We receive none of it.
  • The data remains until you delete it: delete a project, or uninstall the app.

If you enter a contractor’s contact details, they also stay on your phone. They are never sent to us.

3.2 Backup to your iCloud or your Google account

iPhone and iPad. If iCloud is turned on, your data, invoices and quotes included, is synced to your private iCloud, with Apple. Only your Apple Account has access to it.

Android.

  • The phone’s (Google) backup may keep the app’s database and settings, without attachments (photos and files of invoices and quotes). This happens only if the phone encrypts its backups, that is, if it has a screen lock.
  • If you turn it on, automatic backup writes a full copy (a ZIP file, attachments included), at most once a day, to your Google Drive or to the location you choose. Only your account has access to it.

Manual backup (free). A single file contains everything. It is saved wherever you choose.

Who is responsible? These backups are made to your own account, according to your settings. Apple and Google manage them under their own policies. We have no access to them.

Retention period. For as long as you keep them. You delete them from your Apple or Google account settings.

3.3 Sharing with your partner (Costitecture Plus)

What it is for. Tracking a project together, on iPhone and Android, without an account. Inviting requires Costitecture Plus; joining is free.

How it protects your data.

  • The invitation creates a secret key on your phone, drawn at random (256 bits).
  • This key travels only in the QR code or the link shown to your partner. It is placed after the “#” sign, which browsers never send to a server.
  • Anyone who has this QR code or link can join the project. Show it only to the person you choose.
  • The project and its files are encrypted on the phone before being sent (AES-256-GCM, with keys derived by HKDF-SHA256). Neither we nor the hosting provider can read them.
  • Syncing takes place when the app is open.

What passes through the server.

  • The encrypted state of the project, uploaded by each phone.
  • The encrypted invoices, quotes and photos requested by the other phone.
  • The fingerprint (hash) of the sharing access token. The server never keeps the token itself, nor the key.

What the server sees.

  • A sharing number and a device number, generated at random.
  • The identifier of the documents exchanged, the size of the data and the dates of exchange.
  • The IP address, in the hosting provider’s technical logs.

It sees no names, no amounts, no suppliers and no readable invoices.

Legal basis. Performance of the service you request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Retention periods.

3.4 Anonymous usage statistics

Off by default. Early on, the app asks a single question, “Anonymous statistics”, with two equally prominent answers: “Share” and “Don’t share”.

  • For a new user, the question comes after creating their first project.
  • Until you have answered “Share”, nothing is counted or sent.
  • If you were already using a previous version, the question is asked the first time you launch version 2.0. Nothing is sent before you answer. If you had already turned statistics off, they stay off and the question is not asked again.
  • You can change your mind at any time, free of charge: More › Privacy › Anonymous statistics.

What they are for. Knowing how many people use the app, how often, and which features are used most, in order to improve it. Nothing else.

What is sent to the Supabase server (Frankfurt), if you answered “Share”, in the form of a small summary each time you use the app:

  • the type of phone (iPhone or Android), the app version and the region set on the phone (for example “FR”), never your location;
  • the day; whether it is the first opening or the first use of the day;
  • the number of days since the previous use and since installation (counted over 13 months at most);
  • the time spent in the app;
  • the plan used: free, annual Plus or lifetime Plus;
  • counters from a closed list, for example “project created”, “expense added”, “credit note added”, “shopping list item added”, “noted price added”, “comparison quote chosen”, “PDF report”, “sharing turned on”, “Plus offer viewed”, “Plus purchase”.

What is never sent. No amounts or budgets. No invoices, quotes or photos. No names of projects, stages, contractors, items or suppliers. No references, links, addresses or contact details. No text you enter. No phone or user identifier.

Each summary carries only a random number generated for it alone. This number is used to avoid counting the same summary twice if the connection drops.

What is kept. On receipt, the summary is added to daily totals (for example “12 PDF reports on 3 March on iPhone”), then discarded. No row per phone or per person is kept.

Legal basis. Your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and the rules on access to information on the phone). You can withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect data already sent.

3.5 Camera and invoice reading

  • The camera is used only at your request, to photograph or scan your invoices and quotes, and to read your partner’s invitation QR code.
  • Automatic reading (amount, date, supplier) happens on the phone. No image is sent to an outside service to be read.
  • On iPhone and iPad, reading uses Apple’s built-in features (Vision, PDFKit). Nothing is sent.
  • On Android, ML Kit text recognition is built into the app. The document scanner and the QR code reader come from Google Play services. These Google libraries send Google technical diagnostic information, in encrypted form: phone model and version, app version, performance (processing time), error codes, and an identifier specific to each installation. They send neither your invoices, nor the text read, nor your contact details. Google processes this information under its own rules.

3.6 Reminders

Payment and delivery reminders (Costitecture Plus) are notifications scheduled on your phone. They do not go through any server. The phone first asks for your permission to show notifications.

3.7 Shopping list links

  • A link saved in the shopping list opens in your browser, only when you tap it.
  • The website visited sees your visit like any other visit. Its own privacy policy applies.
  • We receive nothing. The app does not add any tracking code to the link.

3.8 Costitecture Plus purchases

  • Purchases are processed by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play, under their own terms. They act as controllers of this data.
  • The app checks on the phone, with the store, whether Plus is active.
  • We receive neither your name, nor your contact details, nor your payment method. We see only sales totals provided by Apple and Google.

3.9 Invitation page and website

  • The invitation page and the app’s website contain no cookies, no statistics and no external scripts. The invitation page sends nothing: the key stays after the “#”.
  • As with any website, the website’s hosting provider sees visitors’ IP addresses in its technical logs.
  • Website hosting provider: GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, United States.

3.10 When you write to us

  • Data. Your email address, your message, and anything you choose to attach to it.
  • Purpose. To reply to you.
  • Legal basis. Our legitimate interest in answering questions (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), or performance of the contract if you are writing about your purchase.
  • Retention period. At most 2 years and 3 months after the last exchange. We do this sorting ourselves once a quarter: conversations whose last message is more than 2 years old are then deleted, including from the deleted items folder. There is no automatic deletion.
  • Email service provider. Microsoft (Outlook.com service), which receives the emails sent to the support address.

Tip: do not send invoices or personal data that are not needed for your question.

3.11 Store statistics

Apple and Google may provide the developer with aggregate, anonymous figures: number of downloads, countries, crash reports. This depends on your phone’s sharing settings. You can change them in your phone’s privacy settings.

3.12 Review requests

The app may invite you to rate it, through the official system of the App Store (Apple) or Google Play. Your rating and review are handled by the store, under its own rules. The app does not receive them.

7. Security

  • All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Sharing with your partner is end-to-end encrypted (256-bit key, AES-256-GCM, keys derived by HKDF-SHA256). The key is never sent to the server, which keeps only the fingerprint of the access token.
  • On the phone, the sharing key is stored in the iPhone Keychain or protected by the Android Keystore. It is excluded from Android backups.
  • The server does not expose its tables. Only a few controlled operations are allowed.
  • The statistics contain no identifier.
  • Nothing is sent from the demo and test versions.

8. Your rights

In all countries

At any time, you can:

  • delete a project in the app, or uninstall the app;
  • delete your backups from your Apple or Google account;
  • stop sharing with your partner: this phone’s encrypted copy is deleted from the server;
  • turn off anonymous statistics: More › Privacy;
  • write to us at discount421@outlook.fr with any question or request.

One limitation, to be completely honest. We have no account, no identifier and no means of linking statistics or a share to a person. We therefore cannot find “your” statistics to show them to you or delete them (Article 11 GDPR). For sharing, the most reliable tool is “Stop sharing” in the app.

How to exercise your rights.

  • Write to discount421@outlook.fr or to 9B rue Antoine Durafour, 42420 Lorette, France.
