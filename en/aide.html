Help and contact

Answers to the most common questions. Something else? Write to us.

Your data

Where is my data?

On your phone. On iPhone and iPad, also in your private iCloud if iCloud is turned on, invoices and quotes included. On Android, also in the phone’s Google backup (without the photos and files of invoices and quotes) and, if you turn it on, in the automatic backup to Google Drive. No account is needed.

I’m changing phones

iPhone to iPhone: your data comes back with iCloud. Android to Android: the Google backup brings back your projects, but not the photos and files of invoices and quotes; to get everything back, restore your automatic backup or a file made with More › Back up now. Between iPhone and Android: More › Back up now, then “Restore a backup” on the new phone.

How do I make a full backup?

More › Back up now: a single file contains your projects, invoices and quotes. It’s free. Keep it somewhere safe; it can be restored on iPhone or Android. On Android, you can also turn on automatic backup to Google Drive.

Self-build

Record a return, credit note or refund

In the expense form, turn on “Credit note or refund”. The amount is then deducted from what you’ve spent, from the stage and from the supplier. You can link it to the original purchase. When you read a credit note, the switch turns on by itself: check before saving.

Record who does a stage

On the stage’s page, choose “Done by”: “Myself”, “Contractor” or “Both”. This is optional.

How are my savings calculated?

With Plus, on the page of a stage done by “Myself” or “Both”, choose a “Comparison quote”: a contractor’s quote for the same work, materials included. The saving is the amount of that quote minus your actual spending on the stage. It’s provisional until the stage is finished. Any extra cost is shown honestly. Without a quote, nothing is estimated.

Using the shopping list

With Plus, add items from a stage’s page or from the shopping list in the Stages tab. For each item, note one or more prices: supplier, price, delivery cost, reference and link. The cheapest total including delivery stands out. “Buy” creates the expense, already filled in.

Share with your partner

Invite my partner

More › Invite my partner (Costitecture Plus): a QR code appears. Your partner scans it with their phone’s camera. If they don’t have the app, they’re guided to the App Store or Google Play; the first time they open it, the project is added. Joining is free.

Join my partner’s project

Scan their QR code with the camera, or in the app: More › “Join my partner’s project”. You can also paste the link they sent you.

When do changes reach the other phone?

Automatically, while the app is open: when it opens, a few seconds after each entry, and every minute while it’s on screen. Invoices and quotes arrive once both phones have opened the app. Update the app on both phones to get the new features.

Stop sharing

More › “Stop sharing on this phone”. The project stays as it is on this phone, but is no longer updated. To share again, create a new invitation: it uses a new key.

Payments and subscription

Payment and delivery reminders

With Plus, the app reminds you the day before each payment and each scheduled delivery. Allow notifications when the app asks, or in your phone’s settings.

How much does Costitecture Plus cost?

There are two options: an annual subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. The exact price, in your currency, is shown in the App Store and Google Play, and on the offer screen in the app.

Recover my Plus purchase

More › Discover Costitecture Plus › “Restore purchases”, with the same Apple Account or the same Google account.

Cancel the annual subscription

iPhone and iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Android: Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. The lifetime purchase has nothing to cancel.

Privacy

Anonymous statistics

They’re off by default. The app asks you a single question; nothing is sent until you’ve agreed. You can change your mind at any time: More › Privacy › Anonymous statistics. They never contain anything you enter.
