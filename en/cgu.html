5. Sharing with your partner and backups

Sharing with your partner (Plus)

  • Inviting requires Costitecture Plus. Joining is free.
  • Sharing is end-to-end encrypted. The key is created on your phone. It travels only in the QR code or the invitation link.
  • Neither we nor the server’s hosting provider can read your project.
  • As a result, we cannot recover anything for you. If the key is lost on all phones, the copy on the server is unreadable. Your project remains on each phone that already had it.
  • Anyone who sees the QR code or the link can join the project. Show it only to the person you choose.
  • A person who has joined cannot be removed remotely. Sharing must be stopped on both phones, and then a new invitation created.
  • Syncing takes place when the app is open on the phones.
  • The server may be temporarily unavailable, for example in the event of an outage or excessive load. Your data then stays on your phone. Syncing resumes later.

Backups

  • iCloud (Apple) or Google backup is made to your own account. It depends on your settings and on Apple’s or Google’s terms. We have no access to it.
  • A manual backup is a file saved wherever you choose.
  • We recommend keeping a backup off your phone. Without a backup, a lost or wiped phone may result in the loss of your data.
